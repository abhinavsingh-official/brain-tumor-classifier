Brain Tumor MRI Classifier

Upload a brain MRI slice to classify it as glioma, meningioma, pituitary tumor, or no tumor, with a Grad-CAM heatmap of what the model looked at.

Demo model retrained on the public Kaggle brain-tumor-mri-dataset, built on top of the preprocessing/architecture approach from Masoud Nickparvar's Brain-Tumor-MRI-Classification project (MIT License). For portfolio/demo purposes only — not a medical device.
